## About

This repository contains structured study notes covering the major concepts of
Artificial Intelligence, from fundamental AI concepts and classical search
techniques to Machine Learning, Neural Networks, Natural Language Processing,
Expert Systems, and the philosophy and ethics of AI.

The notes are organized chapter-wise and include explanations, examples,
algorithms, comparisons, summaries, key points, and questions for revision.
